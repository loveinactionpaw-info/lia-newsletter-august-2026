July 19–25 · Indianapolis

July in Indianapolis

LIA brought the justice work to the PAW Summer Convention. Five workshops covered voting, reentry, education equity, and economic dignity. Our townhall filled the Wabash Ballroom. On Saturday, volunteers joined the Indianapolis Urban League on Indiana Avenue. To everyone who showed up, thank you.

  • Mon – Fri Five Workshops

    Steadfast Foundations, Ballots & Belonging, Beyond the Bars, Schools That Serve, and Livelihood & Dignity.

  • Wednesday Pews to the Polls

    Eight leaders on faith, justice, and the midterm moment, including the Indianapolis Urban League, LIVE FREE USA, and the NAACP.

  • Saturday Backpacks & Block Party

    Back-to-school giveaway with the Indianapolis Urban League, plus haircuts, health resources, and the Kids Zone.
